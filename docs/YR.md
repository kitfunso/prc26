# YR: what moved between 2025 and 2026, and why we cannot correct for it

Generated by `python -m taxi.yr`. A diagnostic. It declares no cell and decides no verdict.

## 1. The exposure

Every model in this campaign trains on 2025 and is scored on 2026, and the holdout is 2025, so nothing measured on it can see a year
shift. `docs/MIX.md` asked this once and found mean arrival taxi-in stable across the years. That grouping was year and month, never
airport. The ranking file keeps a measured taxi-in on all of its arrivals, so the per-airport version is available and is below.

## 3. Whether arrivals can speak for departures

Nothing here has 2026 departure taxi times, so a 2026 departure shift cannot be measured, only inferred from the arrivals. That
inference needs arrival and departure taxi times to move together. Across 2025 they do not. Each airport-month below is one point,
with each direction taken as a deviation from that airport's own year.

| airport | months | correlation | slope |
|---|---|---|---|
| EDDF | 12 | +0.718 | +0.777 |
| EDDM | 12 | -0.275 | -0.846 |
| EGLL | 12 | +0.523 | +1.296 |
| EHAM | 12 | -0.426 | -0.291 |
| LEBL | 12 | +0.326 | +1.411 |
| LEMD | 12 | +0.888 | +0.838 |
| LFPG | 12 | +0.664 | +1.984 |
| LIRF | 12 | +0.501 | +3.276 |
| LSZH | 12 | -0.179 | -0.155 |
| LTFM | 12 | -0.157 | -0.453 |

Pooled over all 120 airport-months the correlation is **+0.043**. The per-airport figures run from
-0.426 to
+0.888 and change sign between
airports, on twelve points each, which is inside the noise of a correlation at that sample size. Taxi-in has no takeoff queue in it;
taxi-out is mostly queue. They share a surface and little else.

## 4. The correction, applied and refused

Fitting each airport's slope on the ten months the holdout does not use, then correcting the holdout by its arrival deviation, loses.

| correction | holdout RMSE | change |
|---|---|---|
| pooled slope | 232.1809 s | -0.0307 s |
| per-airport slope | 234.3628 s | -2.2126 s |

Against MC2's 232.1502 s both lose, the per-airport version by more than the 2 s floor. The bridge does not hold, so the correction
is refused rather than parked.

## 5. Flag F23

The ten airports moved by up to 70 s of arrival taxi-in between 2025 and 2026, in a direction that follows the
month. Whether departures moved with them is unknown and unknowable from what we hold: there is one year of departure labels, and the
only proxy for the second year fails its own test. So the board score carries a year-shift risk that no holdout number reflects and no
correction available to us removes. Every projection from holdout to board in this campaign inherits it. Recorded, not corrected.
