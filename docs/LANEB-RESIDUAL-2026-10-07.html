Reason and limits

The checked January/July and February/March reference predictions place 41.34% of pooled squared error in Lane B, the flights without plan stamps. January includes two extreme LFPG observations; winter also has a concentrated LTFM failure. These label-based findings motivate a test but do not prove that those errors are predictable. The existing mechanism combines classification, donor predictions and a sparse date-cell prior. This experiment asks whether one continuous squared-error correction can learn the remaining conditional error without changing its probability or date rules. It differs from probability recalibration, a new outcome classifier, signed expert gates and changes to Lane A blend weights. Evaluation data has been adaptively reused; this is a development screen, not fresh confirmation.
