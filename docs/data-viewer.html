Taxi-Out Data Viewer

Every row is one aircraft movement at one of ten airports. For departures the target is TAXITIME_SEC_mvt: seconds from off-block at the stand to wheels-up on the runway. The ranking set blanks that column and the off-block time; everything else stays.

Files and months

Twelve monthly training files cover 2025. The ranking file holds January and July 2026, plus a handful of stragglers timestamped 1 August. Departures and arrivals are near 50:50; only departures carry the target.

Twenty-four real rows

Heathrow departures from 06:00Z on Tuesday 15 July 2025, in takeoff order, all 30 columns. Times are UTC and trimmed to the clock. The highlighted column is the target; the two columns marked blank in ranking are what the ranking file removes.

Column catalogue

Coverage is over the 2,085,047 training departures that have a target, and over the 344,841 ranking departures. _mvt columns come from the airport's own records, _flt columns from the Network Manager flight list joined onto them; the join misses about 1.4% of rows, which is why every _flt column sits at the same coverage.

Taxi-out by airport

Heathrow is the slowest airport by a wide margin. Rome has a broken tail: 1,967 taxi-outs longer than an hour and a maximum of 131,167 s, which is 36 hours, against a p90 of 28 minutes. Zurich has 313 zero-or-negative values. Those rows need handling before anything is fit on them.

Bars are one-minute bins, shared x-axis 0 to 45+ minutes, the last bin catches everything longer. Each panel has its own y-scale. The dark tick is the median.

The off-block clue that survives blanking

The Network Manager's own actual off-block time, AOBT_3_flt, is left in the ranking file. Takeoff minus that is a direct guess at taxi-out. This is how far that guess misses the true value, over every 2025 departure that has both.

Takeoff − AOBT_3 − true taxi-out, 30-second bins

The middle is tight but the shoulders are heavy. NM records off-block to the minute and from a different source than the airport, so the two clocks disagree by minutes on a large minority of flights. Used raw this scores about 385 s RMSE over the year; the current leaderboard leader is at 249 s.
