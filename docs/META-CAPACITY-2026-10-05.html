Hypothesis and limits

The inner raw tables fit members on roughly two thirds of the permitted outer scope, while outer models refit all permitted rows. The old residual-stage report in docs/RECAL.md already records the analogous fit-size gap. A fixed set of weights estimated from models trained on about nine tenths of the permitted scope may better reflect the outer member ordering. This tests the meta prediction source, rather than a blend grid, weight shrinkage parameter, new weather gate or first-stage feature family.

Use the saved outer C/L/N selection checkpoints on their common last-tenth stopping dates. These labels already selected iteration/epoch budgets, so the weight-fitting predictions carry stopping-selection bias and cover late-year dates rather than all seasons. This is explicitly adaptive training-only weight estimation, not a clean inner holdout. The separate complete outer files remain excluded from every checkpoint fit, stopping call and new weight/residual fit. A gain must survive both outer blocks and the declared weather/episode gates; no fresh-test claim is allowed.
