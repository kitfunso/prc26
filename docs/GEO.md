# GEO: what airport surface geometry could be worth, priced without fetching it

Generated by `python -m taxi.geo`. Champion MC2 on the lane-A holdout: 339,046 rows at 232.1502 s.
A diagnostic. It declares no cell and decides no verdict.

## 1. Why the question came up

None of the 30 raw columns is a distance, and taxi time is distance over speed. Stand-to-runway distance from open map data is the
largest single piece of physics the model has never been given. Fetching it means matching stand names to map features at ten airports.
That work is only worth starting if the ceiling clears the 2 s floor, and the ceiling can be measured from columns already in hand.

## 2. The bound

Stand-to-runway distance is fixed by the airport, the stand and the runway. Any geometric feature is therefore a function of those three
columns, all three of which MC2 already carries. Its main effect cannot free more than the cell identities still explain in MC2's residuals.
The table removes each cell's own mean residual, then subtracts what the same number of cells removes from shuffled residuals, because
fitting thousands of means to 339,046 rows pays a little by chance alone.

| grouping | cells | raw | chance | honest ceiling | composite |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| airport | 10 | +0.307 s | +0.003 s | **+0.304 s** | +0.215 s |
| airport + runway | 59 | +0.419 s | +0.018 s | **+0.401 s** | +0.284 s |
| airport + stand | 2,422 | +1.944 s | +0.800 s | **+1.140 s** | +0.807 s |
| airport + stand + runway | 9,061 | +5.404 s | +3.043 s | **+2.330 s** | +1.650 s |

So every stand-and-runway geometric feature put together is capped at **+2.330 s of lane A**, or
**+1.650 s of composite**, before one byte of map data is fetched. The 2 s floor is 2 s of lane A.
The cap is measured on the holdout's own rows, so a real feature estimated from training months can only come in under it.

## 3. How much of that cap survives the calendar

The cap assumes the holdout's own cell means are known. A model has to estimate them from the training months and carry them forward.
This is that estimate: each cell's mean out-of-fold residual over the training months, shrunk toward zero by k, applied to the holdout.

| estimator | best k | holdout | gain lane A | composite | share of cap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| stand x runway, mean | 50 | 231.5536 s | +0.5966 s | +0.422 s | 26% |
| stand only, mean | 100 | 231.8473 s | +0.3029 s | +0.214 s | 13% |

The best transferable estimate is **+0.597 s of lane A**, +0.422 s of composite: 26% of the cap.
The rest of the cap is holdout-specific noise that no feature can carry forward. This also explains G1's tie: it attacked the stand level,
which holds less than the pair, with an estimator that lands in the same place.

## 4. Where the cap sits, and the one argument left for geometry

Target encoding shrinks a thin cell toward zero, so it is structurally blind where few training rows back the cell. A distance is not:
it generalises across cells. If the cap sat mostly in thin cells, geometry could reach what the encoding cannot. The bands add to the total.

| training rows behind the cell | holdout rows | share | honest ceiling |
|---|---|---|---|
| under 20 | 5,781 | 1.7% | +0.780 s |
| 20 to 99 | 28,773 | 8.5% | +0.605 s |
| 100 to 499 | 145,607 | 42.9% | +0.637 s |
| 500 or more | 158,885 | 46.9% | +0.299 s |

Thin cells hold a third of the cap on under a fiftieth of the rows, which is the strongest ground the geometry case has. It is still
under a second of lane A on its own.

One channel escapes the bound in section 2. Queue time plausibly scales with distance, and a distance times congestion term is not a
function of the three columns alone. Splitting every cell by congestion tercile prices that channel too.

- cell alone: **+2.330 s** of lane A
- cell by congestion tercile: **+3.295 s**
- so the interaction channel adds **+0.965 s** of lane A, +0.683 s of composite

## 5. What this closes

Everything airport surface geometry could touch, main effect and congestion interaction together, is capped at **+3.295 s of lane A**
or **+2.333 s of composite**, measured with the holdout's own answers in hand. At the 26% rate at which this
kind of structure has been shown to survive the calendar, the realistic figure is near +0.597 s of composite.
The residual gap to first place is 5.4 to 10.1 s of composite. Fetching stand geometry for ten airports cannot close it, and the ceiling
for the whole direction sits under the floor a verdict would have to clear. The direction is priced, not guessed, and it stays closed.
