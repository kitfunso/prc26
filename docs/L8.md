# L8: lane-B learned copy probability, Jan+Jul 2025 development holdout

Train months (2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12). Touched rows as L4 (['LIRF']); every other row keeps L4b. Regenerate with `python -m taxi.l8`. L6a's gated mixture throughout; only P(copy) changes, and it is fit per touched airport on that airport's no-type rows of the fit months. Columns: band one-hot, the stand-occupancy flag, its age in hours (0 when free), hour of day as sine and cosine, and the Nov-Apr flag ((11, 12, 1, 2, 3, 4)). L8a: logistic regression on those, no airline. L8b: L8a's odds times the odds ratio of L5a's shrunk airline x band table (10 pseudo-rows) against the pooled rate. L8c: CatBoost classifier, depth 3, 300 rounds, learning rate 0.05, l2 10, on z_sched, the occupancy flag and age, hour, the winter flag, the band and the airline as a categorical. A cell whose fit rows are all one class returns their mean. Every cell and the champion carry the floor at 0 s (H22). k = 4; the CV winner alone meets the holdout.
